OSA is an Antwerp section of the [UBA](https://www.uba.be/), the Royal Belgian
Amateur Radio Union. Under the section callsign **ON4OSA** we bring together
radio amateurs from the Antwerp region: from licensed operators and shortwave
listeners to anyone fascinated by radio communication and technology.

## Our history

Our section grew out of the CW operators of the legendary Antwerp coastal
station OSA in **1935** — which is also where our section name comes from. That
makes OSA one of the oldest UBA sections in the country. Those telegraphy roots
still shape what we do today, from CW field days to the club station on board
the lightship [West-Hinder 3]({% include url.html slug='on9bd' lang='en' %}{{ page_url }}).
The section now counts more than 50 members.

## What we do

- **Monthly meetings** with talks, technical sessions and shared experience.
- **On-air activity** — contests, field days and special event stations.
- **Homebrew & experimenting** with antennas, receivers and digital modes.
- **Guidance toward your license** for anyone preparing for the HAREC exam
  and working toward their own callsign.
- **QSL cards** — UBA members who registered with section OSA receive their
  QSL cards through us. You can collect them at our monthly meeting, every
  first Thursday of the month.
